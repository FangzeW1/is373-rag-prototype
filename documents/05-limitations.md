# TaskNest Scope and Limitations

TaskNest was developed incrementally.

Its first milestone was a public homepage without accounts or storage. A later milestone added authentication and a member dashboard. Another milestone introduced administrator authorization.

These milestones do not establish that TaskNest has a complete task-management system, production deployment, or persistent schoolwork storage.

The prototype should not be described as a finished commercial application. Future work could include storing assignments, deadlines, and other student planning information.
